The Concept

A new generation of
destination development

Montesol Development is envisioned as a future-oriented destination that brings together hospitality, wellness, residential living and sustainable infrastructure in a Mediterranean landscape — designed for health, longevity and long-term living.

01

Climatic Health

A mild, stable Mediterranean climate that supports recovery, daily activity and a healthier rhythm of living throughout the year.

02

Wellness & Longevity

Spaces and programmes built around preventive health, regeneration and longevity — integrating movement, nutrition and rest.

03

Premium Residences

Quiet, design-led residences for long-stay and permanent living, set within nature and built to a lasting standard of quality.

04

Renewable Energy

Solar, storage and resource-conscious systems planned as core infrastructure to power the destination sustainably.

The Location

A location shaped by climate, landscape and lifestyle

Set within a Mediterranean coastal landscape of sea views, hills and open nature, the site offers calm and privacy while remaining connected to established cities and infrastructure. It is a place defined by clean air, natural light and a slower, healthier pace of life — without sacrificing accessibility or quality of living.

Sustainability

Sustainability as infrastructure, not decoration

The project is designed around the idea that sustainability should be part of the infrastructure from the beginning — energy, water, materials and landscape planning working together as one system rather than added as an afterthought.

Solar & Storage

On-site solar generation paired with storage to cover a meaningful share of the destination's energy demand.

Water Management

Efficient water use, collection and reuse strategies suited to a Mediterranean climate and its seasonal rhythms.

Responsible Materials

A preference for durable, low-impact materials selected for longevity, comfort and a reduced footprint.

Landscape Planning

Native planting and careful integration with the terrain to protect the surrounding nature and microclimate.

Smart Systems

Intelligent building and energy management to optimise comfort and efficiency across the development.

Long-Term Mindset

Decisions guided by long-term value and resilience rather than short-term marketing claims.
